COURSE OUTCOMES (COs) 
After the successful completion of the course the students will be able to: 
CO1 Determine the rank of a matrix to investigate the existence and uniqueness of solutions, identify and 
analyse system behaviour under different conditions. 
CO2 Apply fundamental principles of Eigen values, Eigen vectors, diagonalization and quadratic forms, 
enabling the analysis and interpretation of basic engineering problems. 
CO3 Apply fundamental concepts such as partial derivatives, chain rule, local linear approximations and 
optimization techniques to analyse and solve problems involving functions of two variables. 
CO4 Compute multiple integrals and apply them to find areas and volumes of geometrical shapes, mass and 
centre of gravity of plane laminas. 
CO5 Describe functions using various series expansions such as Taylor series, Binomial series and Fourier 
series.
